Pehla Kadam: Base Samajhiye

Ordinance (अध्यादेश) Kya Hota Hai?

“

Ordinance (अध्यादेश): विश्वविद्यालय (University) ke aantarik niyamon aur up-kanoonon (By-laws) ka ek aadhikarik likhit dastavez hota hai, jo uski shaikshanik (academic), prashasnik (administrative) aur rozmarra ki karya-pranali ko sanchalit karta hai.

Aam bhasha mein kahein toh: College ka koi bhi teacher ya principal apni marzi se niyam nahi badal sakta. Jo cheez University ke Ordinance mein likhi hai, vahi antim kanoon (final law) hai. Agar aapke pass ordinance ka reference hai, toh college aapko be-vajah rok ya dara nahi sakta!

📌 Tip: RGPV ke sabhi ordinances ka official gazette dekhne ke liye aap University ke RGPV Ordinance Repository (www.rgpv.ac.in) par jakar Ordinance No. 33 verify kar sakte hain.

Clause 5.8 • Exit & Degree Award

BCA Degree Milne Ki Condition

Exact Official Text: "A candidate on successfully completion of the first Six semesters with minimum CGPA of 5.0, shall be eligible for the award of a Bachelor Degree of Computer Applications (BCA) Or A candidate on successfully completion of the first Eight semesters with minimum CGPA of 5.0, shall be eligible for the award of a Bachelor Degree of Computer Applications (BCA) with honours."

🎯 Aasaan Bhasha Mein Matlab:

  • Option A (3 Saal baad exit): Agar tum pehle 6 semesters (1st to 6th) successfully pass kar lete ho aur overall CGPA 5.0 ya usse zyada hai, toh tum seedha BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) ki degree claim kar sakte ho.
  • Option B (4 Saal baad exit): Agar tum 8 semesters (4 saal) complete karke nikalte ho with minimum 5.0 CGPA, toh tumhe BCA (Honours) ki degree milegi.
Clause 5.9 • Re-entry & Surrender

Wapas Aakar MCA Pura Karne Ka Option

Exact Official Text: "A candidate who possesses a Bachelor Degree of Computer Applications (BCA) of the university shall be eligible for admission to the seventh semester... provided that immediately after the declaration of the results of the final semester examinations and before conferment of the Degree of Master of Computer Applications the candidate shall surrender to the university the Bachelor Degree of Computer Applications (BCA)..."

🎯 Aasaan Bhasha Mein Matlab:

  • Re-entry Feature: Agar tumne 6th sem ke baad BCA lekar exit kar diya, lekin 1 ya 2 saal baad laga ki "mujhe MCA pura karna hai", toh tum wapas aakar direct 7th semester mein admission le sakte ho! (BCA Honours wale direct 9th sem mein).
  • Sabse Badi Shart (Degree Surrender): Jab tum apna MCA poora pass kar loge, toh MCA ki final degree milne se pehle tumhe apni pehle wali BCA/BCA Honours degree University ko officially wapas (surrender) karni hogi. Ek candidate ko ek hi Integrated course se do separate degrees (BCA aur MCA dono alag-alag) retain karne ki ijazat nahi hoti.

Ground Reality & Bureaucracy

Ab Clause Toh Samajh Gaye, Par Asli Process Kya Hogi?

Rulebook mein likhna aasan hai, par college ke student cell se file pass karwana ek art hai! 6th semester ke baad BCA lekar safe tareeqe se nikalne ke 5 concrete steps:

01

Sabhi Subjects Clear Karna (Zero Backlogs)

1st se lekar 6th semester tak ke sabhi theory aur practical subjects successfully clear hone chahiye. Agar 6th sem tak koi ATKT baaki reh gayi, toh jab tak wo clear nahi hogi, tab tak exit claim nahi kar sakte. Overall CGPA 5.0 ya usse upar maintain hona must hai.

💡 Pro-Tip: Agar 5th sem tak koi back ho, toh 6th sem ke sath special/re-exam dekar pehle hi use clear kar lein.
02

HOD & Principal Ko Formal Application Dena

6th semester ke final exams hone ke turant baad (ya result aate hi), apne Department HOD aur College Principal/Director ke naam ek formal application likhein. Is application mein clearly RGPV Ordinance 33, Clause 5.8 cite karein ki aap 3 saal ke baad BCA degree claim karke exit lena chahte hain.

📄 Humne aapke liye niche readymade, copy-paste format application banai hai.
03

No Dues Clearance Form

Jaise regular pass-out students karte hain, aapko college ka 'No Dues' form lena hoga. Isme alag-alag counters se sign aur stamp lagwana padega:

📚 Central Library (Kitabein wapas) 💰 Accounts Department (Puri fees cleared) 💻 Computer Labs (No damage) 🏢 Hostel / Mess (Agar opted tha) 🏅 Sports / NCC / T&P Cell
04

Consolidated Mark Sheet & PDC Ke Liye Apply

6th semester ka result officially declare hone ke baad, college Student Cell ya direct RGPV Student Portal ke through apni Final Consolidated Mark Sheet aur Provisional Degree Certificate (PDC) ke liye online/offline apply karein.

⭐ Golden Truth: Kisi bhi aage ke admission (jaise B.Ed, MBA, MSc) ya Government Job verification mein PDC hi pehle kaam aati hai kyunki original degree convocation mein aane mein 1 saal lag jata hai.
05

TC (Transfer Certificate) & Migration Certificate Lena

Kyunki aap is college/course se apna academic relation conclude kar rahe hain, aapko college se Transfer Certificate (TC) aur Character Certificate milega. RGPV se Migration Certificate ke liye portal par online apply karna hoga. Yeh kisi bhi doosre college ya university mein admission lene ke liye 100% compulsory hote hain.

🤫

Ground Reality: "Student Section Ke Babu Se Pehle Mil Lo!"

Official document mein toh sirf rule likha hota hai, par practically saara paper-work college ki Student Section Cell hi daudati hai. Hamari pakki advice rahegi ki jab aapka 6th semester chal raha ho, tabhi ek baar shanti se student section ke clerk ya apne HOD se milkar casually pooch lein:

"Sir, humare yahan 6th sem ke baad Ordinance Clause 5.8 ke under BCA degree lene ka kya specific form ya internal circular chalta hai?"

Isse result aane ke time aakhri moment par koi unnecessary bhagam-bhag ya "sir kal aana" wali pareshani nahi hogi.

Realistic Deadlines

Degree Kab Tak Milegi? (Expected Timeline)

Official ordinance document mein exact din nahi likhe hote, par RGPV ke normal administrative cycle ke hisaab se actual timeline aisi rehti hai:

1
Stage 1: Exams ke 1 - 2 Mahine Baad

Result & Consolidated Marksheet

6th semester ke final practicals aur theory exams hone ke 30-60 din baad RGPV portal par result declare hota hai. Iske baad college se 6th sem ki marksheet aur final 3-year consolidated marksheet milti hai.

2
Most Important For Admission
Stage 2: 15 se 30 Din ke Andar

PDC (Provisional Degree Certificate)

Jaise hi result aa jata hai aur aap college ka 'No Dues' clear kar lete hain, aap RGPV student portal se online PDC ke liye apply kar sakte hain. Application ke 15 se 30 din mein PDC aa jati hai.

✅ Note: B.Ed, government recruitment, ya private jobs ke documents verification mein yahi PDC 100% legally maani jati hai!
3
Stage 3: 6 Mahine se 1 Saal+

Original Hard-Copy Degree

Original degree university ke Convocation (दीक्षांत समारोह) mein print hokar aati hai. Isme aksar 6 mahine se 1 saal ya zyada samay lagta hai. Par tension mat lijiye, PDC hone par aapka koi kaam nahi rukta.

Smart Student Checklist

Or Koi Question Jo Mere Man Me Aana Chahiye?

Faisla lene se pehle yeh do sabse critical sawal hain jo har samajhdar student ko apne college administration se clear kar lene chahiye:

⚠️ Sawal 1: Degree Nomenclature

Degree Par Exact Naam Kya Likha Hoga?

College examination cell se yeh confirm karein ki 6th semester ke baad aane wali degree par strictly "Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)" likha hoga ya usme "Exit from Dual Degree" jaisa koi extra remark hoga?

Kyun zaroori hai? B.Ed, CTET, State PSC, aur central government teaching jobs ke document verification mein unhe clear-cut 3-year Bachelor's degree (BCA) chahiye hoti hai. Agar clause 5.8 ke anusar standard BCA degree hai, toh koi problem nahi aayegi.
🚨 Sawal 2: Remaining Fees Trap

Aage Ke Semesters Ki Fees Ka Koi Chakkar Toh Nahi?

Kuch private colleges mein jab koi student beech mein course chhodta hai, toh accounts wale bolte hain: "Aapke chhodne se agle 2 saal ki seat khali ho jayegi, isliye penalty ya fees bhariye."

Kanooni Paksh: RGPV Ordinance 33 mein kisi penalty ya remaining years ki fees mangne ka koi niyam nahi hai! Par No-Dues ke waqt lafda na ho, isliye 5th/6th sem mein hi accounts section se chupke se pata kar lein ki exit karne par No Dues asani se milta hai na.

Frequently Asked Doubts (FAQ)

Agar kisi subject mein backlog aati hai, toh aapko pehle use supplementary/re-exam mein pass karna hoga. Jab tak 1st se 6th sem ke sabhi paper pass nahi hote aur CGPA 5.0 touch nahi karta, tab tak BCA degree generate nahi hogi.

Bilkul haan! Ek baar aapko BCA ki degree aur RGPV se TC/Migration mil gaya, aap ek recognized Bachelor graduate hain. Aap kisi bhi All-India entrance exam (NIMCET, CUET-PG, CAT) ya B.Ed ke liye 100% eligible ho jaate hain.

Haan, jab aapka No Dues complete hota hai, toh admission ke time jo Caution Money (Security Deposit) jama ki thi, wo college accounts section se refund hone ke liye eligible ho jaati hai.
